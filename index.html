Questions

Is the free YouTube content actually available on this website?

Yes. The Free Classroom page is the existing Factorial video portal embedded into this site experience. Students can browse chapters and open lessons without leaving for a separate playlist directory.

Are paid courses replacing the free library?

No. In this prototype the free classroom remains a first-class section. Paid programs are separate optional products with their own checkout flow.

Can the paid checkout become real?

Yes. The course cards currently use a simulated checkout so the prototype works without secrets. A production build can connect the same UI to Razorpay Orders plus server-side payment verification.
